# Background music

Drop audio files here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.flac`) and
switch **Background music** on in the Channel automation panel. Each new short
gets one of these tracks, started partway in, quietly under the stream audio:
about -18 dB, ducking further whenever someone talks, with a fade in and out.

Use music you have the right to post. Copyrighted songs get shorts muted or
taken down on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and can earn the account a
strike. Royalty-free libraries, or tracks you made or licensed, are the safe
choice.

Files in this folder are not committed to git.

`SHORTS_MUSIC_DIR` points somewhere else; `SHORTS_MUSIC_VOLUME` (default
0.12) sets how loud the bed sits before ducking.
